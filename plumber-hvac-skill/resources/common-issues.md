## 1. HVAC COMMON ISSUES

### Air Conditioning Problems

#### AC Not Cooling
**Possible Causes:**
- Dirty air filter restricting airflow
- Low refrigerant (Freon) levels
- Dirty condenser coils (outdoor unit)
- Thermostat set incorrectly
- Circuit breaker tripped
- Compressor failure

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check thermostat: Set to "Cool" and temperature below room temp
2. Replace air filter if dirty
3. Check circuit breaker panel
4. Ensure outdoor unit is running (fan spinning)
5. Clear debris from around outdoor unit

**When to Call:** If basic checks don't resolve, or if you suspect refrigerant leak

---

#### AC Running Constantly
**Possible Causes:**
- Dirty filter restricting airflow
- Refrigerant leak
- Thermostat issues
- Undersized unit for space
- Poor insulation/duct leaks
- Extreme outdoor temperatures

**Quick Checks:**
1. Replace air filter
2. Check thermostat settings and batteries
3. Close windows and doors
4. Check for blocked vents

**When to Call:** If running constantly for more than a few hours on moderate days

---

#### AC Leaking Water
**Possible Causes:**
- Clogged condensate drain line
- Broken condensate pump
- Frozen evaporator coils
- Dirty air filter
- Low refrigerant causing ice

**Quick Checks:**
1. Turn off AC at thermostat
2. Check and replace air filter
3. Locate drain line and check for clogs

**When to Call:** Immediately if water is entering living spaces; soon if minor drip

---

#### AC Making Strange Noises
**Noise Type → Possible Cause:**
- **Buzzing**: Electrical issue, loose parts, failing contactor
- **Clicking**: Debris in fan, electrical relay issues
- **Hissing**: Refrigerant leak, duct leak
- **Grinding**: Motor bearings failing, metal-on-metal
- **Squealing**: Belt issues, motor problems
- **Rattling**: Loose panels, debris in unit

**Quick Checks:**
1. Turn off unit if noise is severe
2. Check for visible loose panels
3. Look for debris in outdoor unit

**When to Call:** Immediately for grinding, buzzing, or hissing sounds

---

### Heating/Furnace Problems

#### Furnace Not Starting
**Possible Causes:**
- Thermostat set incorrectly
- Pilot light out (older units)
- Circuit breaker tripped
- Dirty air filter
- Gas valve closed
- Safety switch triggered

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check thermostat: Set to "Heat" and above room temp
2. Replace air filter
3. Check circuit breaker
4. Verify gas valve is open (parallel to pipe = on)
5. Check furnace door/panel is secure (safety switch)

**When to Call:** If basic checks fail, or if you smell gas

---

#### Furnace Blowing Cold Air
**Possible Causes:**
- Pilot light out
- Gas valve closed
- Dirty flame sensor
- Thermostat fan set to "On" instead of "Auto"
- Unit in delay mode
- Heat exchanger issues

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check thermostat fan setting (should be "Auto")
2. Wait 2-3 minutes (normal delay)
3. Check if pilot is lit (older units)
4. Verify gas supply

**When to Call:** If blowing cold after 5+ minutes of operation

---

#### Furnace Short Cycling (Frequent On/Off)
**Possible Causes:**
- Dirty air filter
- Oversized unit
- Thermostat in poor location
- Flame sensor dirty
- Overheating (limit switch triggering)
- Blocked exhaust flue

**Quick Checks:**
1. Replace air filter
2. Ensure all vents are open
3. Check thermostat location (not near heat sources)

**When to Call:** If cycling continues after filter change

---

#### Furnace Making Strange Noises
**Noise Type → Possible Cause:**
- **Booming**: Delayed ignition (gas buildup)
- **Whistling**: Airflow restriction, duct leak
- **Rattling**: Loose panels, debris
- **Scraping**: Blower wheel issues
- **Humming**: Motor or transformer issues
- **Popping**: Duct expansion/contraction (normal)

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check for loose access panels
2. Replace air filter
3. Note when noise occurs (startup, running, shutdown)

**When to Call:** Immediately for booming sounds; soon for scraping or humming

## 2. PLUMBING COMMON ISSUES

### Water Heater Problems

#### No Hot Water
**Possible Causes:**
- Tripped circuit breaker (electric)
- Pilot light out (gas)
- Failed heating element (electric)
- Broken thermocouple (gas)
- Sediment buildup
- High demand exceeding capacity

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check circuit breaker (electric)
2. Verify pilot is lit (gas)
3. Check gas valve is open (gas)
4. Reset high-temperature limit switch (red button on thermostat)

**When to Call:** If basic checks don't restore hot water within an hour

---

#### Water Not Hot Enough
**Possible Causes:**
- Thermostat set too low
- Sediment buildup in tank
- Dip tube broken
- Undersized heater for demand
- Cross-connected hot/cold lines

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check thermostat setting (should be 120°F)
2. Drain some water to check for sediment
3. Verify only hot water is running

**When to Call:** If adjusting thermostat doesn't help

---

#### Water Heater Leaking
**Location → Possible Cause:**
- **Top**: Inlet/outlet connections, T&P valve
- **Bottom**: Drain valve, internal tank failure
- **Side**: Internal tank failure (replace needed)
- **T&P Valve**: Excessive pressure/temperature

**Quick Checks:**
1. Turn off power/gas to heater
2. Turn off water supply if leaking heavily
3. Check if leak is from valve (may just need tightening)

**When to Call:** Immediately for any significant leak; side leaks mean replacement needed

---

#### Discolored/Rusty Hot Water
**Possible Causes:**
- Anode rod depleted
- Sediment buildup
- Corroding tank
- Rusty pipes

**Quick Checks:**
1. Flush water heater to remove sediment
2. Check if only hot water is affected
3. Run water for several minutes to see if it clears

**When to Call:** If flushing doesn't resolve; may need anode rod replacement or new heater

---

### Drain Problems

#### Slow Draining Sink
**Possible Causes:**
- Hair/debris buildup
- Grease accumulation
- Partial clog in P-trap
- Venting issues

**Quick Fixes:**
1. Remove and clean P-trap
2. Use plunger
3. Try drain snake/auger
4. Avoid chemical drain cleaners (damage pipes)

**When to Call:** If multiple fixtures slow, or DIY methods fail

---

#### Completely Clogged Drain
**Possible Causes:**
- Solid object blockage
- Severe grease buildup
- Tree root intrusion (main line)
- Collapsed pipe

**Quick Checks:**
1. Try plunger first
2. Check other drains (isolated vs. whole house)
3. Note any gurgling sounds

**When to Call:** If plunger doesn't work, or multiple drains affected

---

#### Sewer Odors
**Possible Causes:**
- Dry P-trap (evaporated water)
- Cracked sewer line
- Blocked vent stack
- Damaged wax ring (toilet)

**Quick Checks:**
1. Run water in all drains (refill P-traps)
2. Check toilet for rocking (wax ring issue)
3. Note when odor is strongest

**When to Call:** If odors persist after running water; could indicate serious venting or line issues

---

### Toilet Problems

#### Running Toilet
**Possible Causes:**
- Flapper not sealing
- Fill valve malfunction
- Float set too high
- Chain too long/short
- Overflow tube issue

**Quick Fixes:**
1. Adjust float height
2. Check chain length (should have slight slack)
3. Clean or replace flapper
4. Replace fill valve assembly

**When to Call:** If adjustments don't stop running; wasting water and money

---

#### Toilet Won't Flush
**Possible Causes:**
- Handle/chain disconnected
- Low water in tank
- Clogged drain holes in bowl
- Main line blockage

**Quick Checks:**
1. Check handle connection to flapper
2. Ensure tank fills properly
3. Try plunger
4. Check other drains (whole house backup?)

**When to Call:** If simple fixes don't work, or sewage backing up

---

#### Toilet Leaking at Base
**Possible Causes:**
- Failed wax ring
- Loose tee bolts
- Cracked toilet base
- Condensation (sweating)

**Quick Checks:**
1. Confirm it's actually leaking (not condensation)
2. Check if toilet rocks (loose bolts)
3. Look for water on floor after flushing

**When to Call:** Soon—water damage to subfloor can be expensive

---

### Pipe Problems

#### Burst Pipe
**Immediate Actions:**
1. **SHUT OFF MAIN WATER IMMEDIATELY**
2. Turn off water heater
3. Open faucets to drain remaining water
4. Call emergency plumber
5. Document damage for insurance

**Prevention:**
- Insulate pipes in unheated areas
- Keep cabinet doors open in extreme cold
- Let faucets drip during freezes
- Know location of main shut-off

---

#### Low Water Pressure
**Possible Causes:**
- Aerator clogged (single fixture)
- Pressure reducing valve failing (whole house)
- Hidden leak
- Municipal supply issue
- Corroded pipes

**Quick Checks:**
1. Clean faucet aerators
2. Check if one fixture or whole house
3. Look for signs of leaks
4. Ask neighbors if they have same issue

**When to Call:** If affecting whole house, or sudden pressure drop

---

#### Water Hammer (Banging Pipes)
**Possible Causes:**
- Loose pipe straps
- Failed air chambers
- High water pressure
- Quick-closing valves

**Quick Fixes:**
1. Secure loose pipes
2. Install water hammer arrestors
3. Check water pressure (should be 40-80 PSI)

**When to Call:** If banging is severe or persistent

## 3. DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS TO ASK CUSTOMERS

### For All Calls
- When did the problem start?
- Is this the first time this has happened?
- Has anyone else looked at this issue?
- What have you tried so far?
- Are there any unusual sounds/smells?
- Is the problem constant or intermittent?

### HVAC-Specific
- What temperature is the thermostat set to?
- What mode is it set to (Heat/Cool/Auto)?
- When was the last filter change?
- Is the outdoor unit running?
- Are all vents open?
- Any rooms warmer/cooler than others?
- Recent utility bill changes?

### Plumbing-Specific
- Is the problem at one fixture or multiple?
- Hot water, cold water, or both?
- Any recent plumbing work done?
- Where is the main water shut-off?
- Age of home and plumbing system?
- Any water discoloration?
- Recent increase in water bill?

## 4. WHEN TO CALL A PROFESSIONAL

### Call Immediately (Emergency)
✓ Burst pipe or major leak
✓ Gas leak smell (also call gas company)
✓ Sewage backup into home
✓ No heat in winter with vulnerable occupants
✓ Electrical burning smell from HVAC
✓ Water heater leaking from tank body
✓ Complete AC failure during heat wave with vulnerable occupants

### Call Within 24 Hours
✓ No hot water
✓ Toilet overflowing won't stop
✓ Multiple drains clogged
✓ Furnace making booming sounds
✓ AC leaking significant water
✓ Pilot light won't stay lit

### Schedule Routine Service
✓ Slow drains
✓ Minor leaks/drips
✓ Unusual noises (not emergency-level)
✓ Reduced efficiency/higher bills
✓ Annual maintenance due
✓ System older than 10 years acting up

## 5. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST

### Monthly Tasks
□ Check HVAC air filter (replace if dirty)
□ Test smoke and CO detectors
□ Check for visible leaks under sinks
□ Ensure water heater area is clear
□ Verify all drains flowing normally

### Quarterly Tasks
□ Inspect HVAC outdoor unit for debris
□ Check water pressure (should be 40-80 PSI)
□ Test GFCI outlets in wet areas
□ Inspect washing machine hoses
□ Check toilet tanks for running water

### Semi-Annual Tasks
□ Professional HVAC tune-up (Spring & Fall)
□ Drain water heater sediment
□ Inspect sump pump operation
□ Clean faucet aerators
□ Check caulking around tubs/showers

### Annual Tasks
□ Replace HVAC air filters regularly
□ Professional drain cleaning if prone to clogs
□ Inspect well pump and pressure tank (if applicable)
□ Have chimney/flue inspected (if gas furnace)
□ Review water heater anode rod
□ Test main water shut-off valve operation

### Seasonal Tasks

**Spring:**
□ Schedule AC tune-up
□ Clear debris from outdoor units
□ Check irrigation system for leaks
□ Inspect roof flashing around vents

**Fall:**
□ Schedule furnace tune-up
□ Insulate exposed pipes before freeze
□ Drain and winterize outdoor faucets
□ Check weatherstripping on doors/windows

**Winter:**
□ Monitor for frozen pipes
□ Keep thermostat set consistently
□ Change filters more frequently
□ Keep vents clear of furniture/rugs

**Summer:**
□ Monitor AC performance
□ Check humidity levels
□ Clean condensate drain line
□ Shade outdoor AC unit (without blocking airflow)

## QUICK REFERENCE: SHUT-OFF LOCATIONS

### Main Water Shut-Off
**Common Locations:**
- Basement or crawlspace, front of house
- Garage, near water heater
- Outside near street (in meter box)
- Utility room

**How to Operate:**
- Wheel valve: Turn clockwise until tight
- Lever valve: Turn perpendicular to pipe

### Water Heater Shut-Off
- Cold water inlet valve on top of heater
- Gas valve near bottom (gas units)
- Circuit breaker (electric units)

### Individual Fixture Shut-Offs
- Under sinks (two valves: hot and cold)
- Behind toilets (single valve)
- At washing machine (two valves)

### Gas Shut-Off
- At meter (outside)
- At appliance (flexible line valve)
- **Only operate if trained; call gas company if unsure**

### Electrical Panel
- Main breaker shuts off entire house
- Individual breakers for specific circuits
- Label all breakers for easy identification
