## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Subject Line Tips
- Keep under 50 characters when possible
- Personalize with customer name or location
- Use emojis sparingly (1 max for most emails)
- Create urgency without being spammy
- A/B test different approaches

### Design Guidelines
- Mobile-responsive templates
- Clear hierarchy with headers
- Bullet points for scannability
- Prominent call-to-action buttons
- Consistent branding (colors, logo, fonts)

### Timing Recommendations
- Transactional emails: Send immediately
- Reminders: 24-48 hours before appointment
- Follow-ups: 2-3 days after service
- Marketing: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM
- Seasonal: 4-6 weeks before peak season

### Compliance
- Include physical mailing address
- Provide unsubscribe option
- Honor opt-outs promptly
- Don't share customer data
- Follow CAN-SPAM Act requirements

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate (aim for 20%+)
- Click-through rate (aim for 3%+)
- Conversion rate (appointments booked)
- Unsubscribe rate (keep under 1%)
- Bounce rate (keep under 2%)
