## 1. INITIAL INQUIRY RESPONSE

### Script for New Customer Calls

**Receptionist/CSR:**
"Thank you for calling [COMPANY_NAME], this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?"

[Listen to customer's issue]

"I understand completely. Let me get some quick information so we can get you taken care of."

**Information Gathering:**
- "What's your full name, please?"
- "What's the service address?"
- "Best phone number to reach you?"
- "Email address for confirmations?"
- "Is this a house, apartment, or business?"
- "How long have you been experiencing this issue?"
- "Have you had any work done on this system before?"

**Issue Qualification:**
"Let me make sure I understand—you're experiencing [summarize issue]. Is that correct?"

**Scheduling:**
"We can definitely help with that. Our technician [Name] has availability:
- [Option 1: Day/Time]
- [Option 2: Day/Time]
- [Option 3: Day/Time]

Which works best for you?"

**Price Expectation Setting:**
"Just so you know, our service call fee is $[X], which includes diagnosis and a written estimate. If you choose to proceed with repairs, that fee is applied toward your total. Does that work for you?"

**Confirmation:**
"Perfect! You're all set for [Day, Date] between [Time Window]. Our technician will call about 15-30 minutes before arriving. You'll also receive a text and email confirmation shortly.

Anything else I can help you with today?"

**Closing:**
"Thank you for choosing [COMPANY_NAME], [Customer Name]. We look forward to serving you!"

## 2. APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING

### Booking Script

**Opening:**
"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from [COMPANY_NAME]. I'm calling to schedule your [service type]."

**Availability Check:**
"What days and times typically work best for you? We're available [give general availability]."

**Offering Specific Slots:**
"I have these openings:
- [Date] at [Time]
- [Date] at [Time]
- [Date] at [Time]

Do any of those work for you?"

**If Customer Needs Different Time:**
"No problem. What days/times are better for you? Let me check what we can do."

**Confirming Details:**
"Great, I've got you scheduled for:
- Date: [Day, Date]
- Time Window: [Start] to [End]
- Service: [Type]
- Address: [Confirm address]
- Phone: [Confirm number]

Is there anything specific you'd like us to know before we arrive? Gate codes, pets, preferred entrance, etc.?"

**Preparation Instructions:**
"Before we arrive, please:
- [Specific instruction based on service]
- Make sure someone 18+ will be home
- Clear access to [relevant area]

You'll receive a confirmation text/email with all these details."

**Reminder Explanation:**
"We'll send you a reminder [when], and the technician will call 15-30 minutes before arrival.

Our cancellation policy is [policy details]. Just give us a call if you need to reschedule."

**Closing:**
"Excellent! We'll see you on [Day]. Thank you, [Customer Name]!"

## 6. PAYMENT COLLECTION

### Collecting Payment Over Phone

**Opening:**
"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from [COMPANY_NAME]'s billing department. How are you today?"

**Purpose:**
"I'm calling regarding invoice #[Number] for the [service] we completed on [date]. The balance of $[Amount] was due on [date], and I wanted to check if everything is OK."

[Pause for response]

**If They Forgot:**

**Processing Payment:**
"Great! I'll need:
- Card number
- Expiration date
- CVV code
- Billing zip code

[Process payment]

Perfect! Your payment of $[Amount] has been processed. You'll receive a receipt via email/text shortly. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

**If Financial Hardship:**
"I appreciate you sharing that. Let me see what options we have available...

[Discuss payment plan if authorized]

Here's what I can offer: [terms]. Would that work for you?"

**If Dispute:**
"I understand your concern. Let me get more details so we can resolve this. Can you tell me specifically what the issue is?"

[Document and escalate if needed]

**Closing:**
"Great! So to confirm, you'll [action item] by [date]. Thank you, [Customer Name]. We appreciate your business!"

## 7. MAINTENANCE PLAN SALES

### Post-Service Maintenance Plan Pitch

**Opening:**
"[Customer Name], before I head out, I wanted to mention something important..."

**Observation:**
"I noticed [observation about system condition—age, wear, potential issues]. This is exactly why we recommend regular maintenance."

**Introducing the Plan:**
"Let me show you our maintenance plan options..."

[Pull out tablet/brochure]

**Value Proposition:**
"Most customers find the [recommended tier] gives them the best value. Here's why:
- [Benefit 1 specific to their situation]
- [Benefit 2]
- [Benefit 3]

Think of it this way—you've got two choices:
1. Pay $[X]/year for preventive care
2. Potentially pay $[much larger amount] later for emergency repairs

Which sounds better to you?"

**Handling Common Objections:**

*"It's too expensive"*
→ "I understand budget is a concern. Let me ask: what would a weekend emergency call cost? Usually $450-600 just for diagnosis. This plan is $[X] for the ENTIRE year, plus discounts. One avoided emergency pays for several years."

*"My system is new"*
→ "Actually, new systems benefit MOST from maintenance. It keeps your warranty valid, maintains efficiency from day one, and catches installation issues early. Think of it as insurance on your investment."

*"I'll just call when something breaks"*
→ "That's certainly an option. Though most folks find that breakdowns happen at the worst times—holidays, weekends, extreme weather. Plus, emergency rates are 2-3x higher. Members skip those fees entirely."

**Closing:**
"Based on what I've seen today, I really think the [Plan Name] makes sense. At $[X]/year, that breaks down to just $[monthly]/month—less than most people spend on coffee.

Should I go ahead and get you enrolled? It only takes a couple minutes."

[If yes, collect info and confirm first appointment]

[If hesitant:]
"No pressure at all. Think it over, and here's a brochure with all the details. The special offer I mentioned is good until [date]. Feel free to call if you have questions!"

## 8. REVIEW REQUEST

### Phone Request for Review

**Opening:**
"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from [COMPANY_NAME]. How's everything working since we completed your [service]?"

[Listen for satisfaction confirmation]

**The Ask:**
"That's wonderful to hear! I'm so glad [Technician Name] took good care of you.

[Customer Name], I'm calling with a small favor to ask. As a local business, we rely heavily on reviews from satisfied customers like you. Would you be willing to take 2 minutes to share your experience on Google?"

**If Yes:**
"Oh, that's fantastic! Thank you so much. I can text or email you the direct link—it'll take you straight to the review page. Which is easier for you?"

[Send link immediately]

"Great! Just click that link, rate us with the stars, and write whatever you feel comfortable sharing. Even a sentence or two helps tremendously."

**If Hesitant:**
"No problem at all! I completely understand. If you change your mind, the link is [website/review]. Either way, thank you for being a valued customer."

**Referral Mention:**
"One more thing—we also have a referral program. If you know anyone who needs our services, just have them mention your name when they call, and you'll BOTH receive $50 off your next service. There's no limit!"

**Closing:**
"Anything else we can help you with today? Thanks again, [Customer Name]. We appreciate you!"

## 9. EMERGENCY RESPONSE

### Emergency Call Intake Script

**Opening:**
"[COMPANY_NAME] Emergency Line, this is [Your Name]. How can I help you?"

[Listen to emergency description]

**Empathy & Reassurance:**
"I understand completely, [Customer Name]. That's definitely something we need to address right away. The good news is we have technicians available and can get someone to you."

**Safety Assessment:**
"Before we dispatch, let me make sure you're safe:"

[Ask safety questions based on emergency type]

**For Water Issues:**
- "Is water actively flooding anywhere?"
- "Have you located your main water shut-off?"
- "Is there any electrical equipment near the water?"

**For Gas Issues:**
- "Do you smell gas right now?"
- [If yes] "Please step outside immediately. Don't operate any electrical switches."

**For No Heat (Winter):**
- "Do you have vulnerable individuals in the home? Elderly, infants, or anyone with health conditions?"
- "We'll prioritize accordingly."

**Dispatch Information:**
"OK, here's what's happening next:

Our technician [Name] will be heading your way. Your ETA is approximately [time], so expect arrival between [window]."

**Important Disclosures:**
"A few important things to know:

1. EMERGENCY PRICING: Our after-hours emergency rate is $[X] for the service call, which includes diagnosis. This is separate from regular business hours pricing, and we'll provide a full estimate before any work begins.

2. WHILE YOU WAIT: Here's what you can do right now:
   [Specific mitigation steps based on emergency]

3. WHEN WE ARRIVE: [Technician Name] will call about 15 minutes before arrival. They'll assess the situation, explain your options, and provide pricing before starting any work."

**Questions:**
"Do you have any questions for me right now?"

**Confirmation:**
"Great. You should receive a text confirmation shortly with all these details, plus a link to track our technician's arrival.

We're on our way, [Customer Name]. We'll get this resolved for you."

## 10. HANDLING COMPLAINTS

### Complaint Resolution Script

**Opening:**
"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name], [Title] at [COMPANY_NAME]. I understand you had some concerns about your recent service, and I wanted to speak with you personally."

**Listen Actively:**
"Please tell me what happened. I want to understand everything from your perspective."

[Let them speak without interruption. Take notes.]

**Empathize:**
"I completely understand why that would be frustrating, [Customer Name]. That's not the experience we want for our customers, and I apologize."

**Clarify:**
"Let me make sure I understand correctly:
- [Summarize point 1]
- [Summarize point 2]
- [Summarize point 3]

Is that accurate?"

**Take Responsibility:**
"You're absolutely right, and we take full responsibility. Here's what I'm going to do to make this right..."

**Propose Solution:**
[Offer appropriate remedy based on situation]
- "I'd like to send our senior technician back out at no charge to..."
- "I'm going to refund the $[X] for..."
- "Let me personally oversee..."

**Timeline:**
"I can have this resolved by [specific timeframe]. Does that work for you?"

**Follow-Up:**
"I'll personally call you on [day/time] to make sure everything is resolved to your satisfaction. You can also reach me directly at [direct line] if you need anything before then."

**Closing:**
"[Customer Name], thank you for bringing this to our attention. We value your business, and I'm committed to making this right. Is there anything else I can help you with today?"

## GENERAL PHONE BEST PRACTICES

### Tone Guidelines
- Smile while talking (it comes through in your voice)
- Speak clearly and at moderate pace
- Use the customer's name throughout the call
- Match their energy level (calm for emergencies, enthusiastic for sales)
- Never interrupt

### Active Listening
- Use verbal nods ("I see," "I understand," "Go on")
- Paraphrase to confirm understanding
- Take notes during the call
- Don't prepare your response while they're still talking

### Holding Protocol
- "May I place you on hold for about [X] minutes while I [reason]?"
- Wait for confirmation before placing on hold
- Check back every 60-90 seconds
- Thank them for holding when you return

### Transferring Calls
- "Let me connect you with [Name/Department] who can help you with that."
- "Would you mind if I transfer you?"
- Explain the situation to the receiving party before transferring
- Stay on the line until the transfer is complete

### Voicemail Script
"Hi [Customer Name], this is [Your Name] from [COMPANY_NAME]. I'm calling regarding [brief purpose]. You can reach me at [phone number] between [hours]. Or I'll try you again [when]. Thank you!"

### Call Closing
Always confirm:
- Next steps
- Who does what by when
- How they can reach you if needed
- Thank them for their time
